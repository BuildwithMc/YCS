# Product Requirements Document: Your Corporate Stylist

## 1. Product Overview
**Your Corporate Stylist** is a premium digital platform for Victory Ezeji's professional styling brand. It positions style as "Wardrobe Architecture" to help executive women and professionals align their visual identity with their professional goals.

## 2. Target Audience
- Executive women and female leaders.
- Entrepreneurs pitching investors.
- Recent graduates entering the job market.
- Rising professionals seeking "Executive Presence".

## 3. Core Features & Functional Requirements

### 3.1. Landing Page (Experience First)
- **Storytelling Hero**: Clear value proposition ("Style as Strategy").
- **Brand Pillars**: ROI of Style, Architectural Approach, Executive Presence.
- **Dynamic Carousel**: Moving styling inspirations and high-end professional outfits.

### 3.2. Services & Pricing Calculator
- **Interactive Selection**: Users can select multiple services (Guides, Live Chats, Accelerators).
- **Real-time Pricing**: Dynamic calculation of the total cost (NGN).
- **Service Details**: Individual sections for each major guide/program.

### 3.3. Gallery & Recommendations
- **Interactive Grid**: A curated lookbook of professional outfits.
- **Popup Lightbox**: detailed views of recommended outfits with "Try This On" styling tips.

### 3.4. Booking & Checkout Flow
- **Step-based form**:
    1. Selection of services.
    2. Contact information (Name, Email, WhatsApp).
    3. Session Details: Day, Time, and Location (In-Studio vs. Virtual).
- **Payment Integration**: Paystack integration for seamless Nigerian-focused payments.
- **Confirmation**: Integrated feedback upon successful booking.

### 3.5. Community & Engagement
- **Newsletter**: Subscription form in the footer.
- **Community Link**: Prominent Telegram community link.
- **Free Consultation**: Calendly integration for a 30-min discovery call.

## 4. Technical Specifications
- **Frontend**: React (Vite), Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion.
- **Backend/Storage**: Google Sheets integration (simulated/implemented) for capturing booking data.
- **Payment Gateway**: Paystack.
- **Icons**: Lucide React.
- **Animations**: motion/react for smooth luxury transitions.

## 5. Visual Identity & Design
- **Palette**: Neutrals (Cognac Brown, Navy Blue, Slate Grey, Cream/Beige).
- **Typography**: Editorial Serif (Playfair Display) paired with clean Sans-Serif (Inter).
- **Mood**: High-end fashion magazine meets professional authority.

## 6. Project Roadmap
- **Phase 1**: Brand identity and landing page development.
- **Phase 2**: Service selection and pricing logic.
- **Phase 3**: Booking flow and payment simulation.
- **Phase 4**: Gallery and about us storytelling sections.
- **Phase 5**: Final integrations (Calendly, Telegram, Newsletter).
